# Species Distribution Modeling of Blue Catfish (*Ictalurus furcatus*)

## Project Overview
This project applies Species Distribution Modeling (SDM) to predict the habitat suitability of blue catfish (Ictalurus furcatus) in the Southeastern United States. The analysis compares two modeling techniques: **Generalized Linear Model (GLM)** and **Logistic Regression**.

## Objective
The goal is to identify areas in the Southeastern U.S. where blue catfish are most likely to establish and spread, based on environmental variables such as climate and elevation.

## Methodology
### Data Sources:
  - **Occurrence data** retrieved from the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)
  - **Environmental layers** (Bioclimatic data) retrieved from WorldClim

### Analysis Methods:
  - Species Distribution Modeling using GLM and Logistic Regression models to predict habitat suitability for blue catfish
  - Models are trained using presence-absence data and environmental variables
  - Models are evaluated based on their ability to predict habitat suitability and are visualized through maps

## Requirements
- R version 4.2.0 or higher
- R Packages: sf, raster, ggplot2, sdm, terra, rgdal, tidyverse, ggspatial, ggmap, rnaturalearth, rnaturalearthdata, rgbif, terra, geodata, sdmpredictors, stringr, stars, elevatr, tigris, ggsflabel, data.table, predicts, tibble, tidyterra

## Results
- Model outputs include maps showing habitat suitability for blue catfish across the Southeastern U.S.
- The maps are visualized using the two SDM models.
